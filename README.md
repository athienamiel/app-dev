# My Favorite Series : The Walking Dead

**The Walking Dead** is a post-apocalyptic horror drama series that follows a group of survivors fighting to stay alive and hold onto their humanity in a world overrun by flesh-eating zombies known as *walkers*.

## Why I like it
* Intense survival instinct and storylines
* Deep character development each season
* Explores the human nature in extreme situations
  
## Main Characters
1. Rick Grimes
2. Daryl Dixon
3. Michonne
4. Carl Grimes
5. Carol Peletier

> "We are the walking dead."
